HOW TO PUBLISH ON NETLIFY
1. Extract MDH_Website.zip on your computer.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Drag the extracted MDH_Website folder (or its contents) onto the Netlify Drop area.
4. Wait for deployment. Netlify will provide your live website URL.
5. Create/verify your Netlify account and check Site configuration > Forms.
6. Submit a test form after the site is live. Form submissions should appear under Forms in your Netlify dashboard.
7. Connect a custom domain later if you have one.

IMPORTANT NOTES
- BOP is shown as a payment method, but the full bank account number from the screenshot is deliberately NOT published on the public website. Ask visitors to request verified bank details through the official WhatsApp number.
- Course fees other than the stated PKR 3,000 training-to-work program are requested through WhatsApp.
- The PKR 3,000 program is described transparently as paid training, not a guaranteed salaried job. Confirm the program duration, scope and work-allocation terms before accepting payment.
- Investment content is educational and does not promise returns.
- Netlify Forms only works after deployment to Netlify; local file preview will not collect submissions.
- Update any course/service details, policies, or program conditions in index.html before launch if needed.
